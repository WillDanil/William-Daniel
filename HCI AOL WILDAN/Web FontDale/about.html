Our Origin Story

FontDale

In 2023, Franz Diego envisioned a destination that would redefine the shopping mall experience. More than retail, FontDale was imagined as a vibrant lifestyle hub where people could gather, connect, and create memories.

Located in Arcadia Circle, FontDale was carefully designed with premium architecture, open spaces, and curated experiences that combine fashion, dining, entertainment, and community.

Since opening in 2025, FontDale has become one of the city's most visited destinations, welcoming thousands of guests each month.

Our Founder

Franz Diego believed that modern shopping destinations should inspire people beyond retail experiences.

Drawing inspiration from world-class lifestyle districts, he envisioned a destination where luxury, culture, and community seamlessly come together.

Through dedication and innovation, FontDale became a symbol of contemporary urban living.
